## What does "Stealth" mean?

A stealth model is being tested anonymously, under a codename, before its official release. It won't reveal its real name or maker, so its self-portrait often riffs on it's own codename instead.

## What are the Thoughts?

The model's own unprompted follow up of its painting, often a description.
