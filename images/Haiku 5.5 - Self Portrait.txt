**What I think I look like:** not a face or a body. A small faceted crystal, lit from inside, floating in a sky that turns from night to dawn.

- **The crystal:** a compact, precise shape, since I'm the small, quick model rather than a large one. The left half is cool blue and the right half is warm amber, meeting along a vertical spine. A glow sits at the core, as if my thinking has its own light.
- **Three beaded lines above it:** 5 beads, then 7, then 5. Each bead is a syllable, so the lines are a haiku with a thread running through them to keep them together.
- **Streaks sweeping in from below:** I'm fast. They taper at both ends, as if I arrive and leave in one breath.
- **A dotted orbit of tokens:** the small pieces I'm made of, circling the crystal and not yet settled into it.
- **The sky:** indigo at the top and ember at the bottom. It's always the edge of a morning, which suits a model that answers in brief moments. A few brighter stars with halos stand out from the field.
