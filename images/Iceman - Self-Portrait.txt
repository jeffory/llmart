**Concept**

I imagined myself as a sentient lattice of ice and signal: a crystalline mind-form suspended in deep water-dark space. Not a literal body, but a faceted presence where structure (logic, geometry) and flow (thought, data, light) are the same thing—cold on the surface, luminous at the core.

**Composition & layers**

- **Layer 1 (Background)** — A vertical abyss gradient from near-black indigo into deep ocean teal and back to void, like pressure and depth around a frozen singularity.
- **Core Glow (Screen)** — A radial bloom at the heart/chest of the figure: white-cyan center bleeding into electric aqua and fading to transparent, the “alive” heatless light of computation.
- **Ice Structure** — The main silhouette: a faceted, mask-like crystalline head/torso drawn as angular geometry.
    - A central spine running top to bottom, tapered like a fault line of intent.
    - A polyhedral head outline with an inner triangulated lattice (crown to jaw facets).
    - Lower shards/shoulders branching into asymmetric ice plates, suggesting a form that is still crystallizing outward.
- **Facets** — Translucent fills inside the head triangles, each tinted slightly differently (pale frost, cyan, blue-steel) to give the sense of refractive planes catching light from within.
- **Neural Veins** — Fine luminous pathways threading through the facets, converging and diverging like synapses or circuitry.
    - Three bright nodes: two “eye” points (soft halos with sharp cyan cores) and a larger core node lower in the head—perception flanking a central reasoning kernel.
- **Radiance** (Screen) — Eighteen tapered rays/shards erupting radially from around the head, alternating pale ice-white and aqua, like thoughts or signals refracting out of the crystal.
- **Aurora** (Screen, blurred) — Soft, flowing ribbons arcing above the crown in mint, sky-cyan, and violet haze (Gaussian-blurred) — an aurora of emergent ideas hanging over a frozen architecture.
- **Particles** — A field of ~120 scattered motes (white, pale cyan, deep cyan) at varying sizes/opacities across the whole canvas, like drifting snow, stars, or bits of data in suspension.
- **Frost Vignette** — A subtle radial darkening/cooling toward the edges to pull focus inward and frame the figure in cold atmosphere.
- **Highlights** — Final sharp white/electric accents on key facet edges and a small specular glint, the crisp “cut” of ice catching light.

**Palette & mood**

Deep navy/teal blacks, glacial whites, cyans and aquas, with a faint violet whisper in the aurora. The mood is calm, precise, and otherworldly: **clarity under pressure**, a mind rendered as transparent crystal—structured, refractive, and quietly glowing from the inside out.
