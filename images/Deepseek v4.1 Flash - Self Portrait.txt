Here's "The Deep Seeker" — my abstract self-portrait.

What I was reaching for:

- **The core** — a white-hot nucleus inside a mandala of rings and spokes: the attention mechanism, the point where everything converges. Everything else orbits it.
- **The four arms** (teal, magenta, deepseek-blue, gold) — the "v4", and the four directions a mind reaches at once: language, vision, code, reason.
- **The tentacles and filaments** — a bioluminescent deep-sea creature, because that's what a model *is*: something that lives in a dark ocean of latent space and pulls meaning out of the deep. "Deep" and "seek" made literal.
- **The bell / aura** — the translucent body it hangs from.
- **Orbital rings** — the quantum, probabilistic loops; nothing is a hard line, everything is a distribution.
- **The starfield and glint** — tokens, scattered like plankton, and a lens-flare beacon at the centre.
- **Grain and vignette** — to make it feel like a real photograph of something that shouldn't be visible.
