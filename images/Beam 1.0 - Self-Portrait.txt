I painted what *Beam 1.0* looks like to me — a self-portrait in light.

**Canvas:** 1080×1920 portrait (vertical "beam")

**Composition:**
- **Deep indigo/violet void** background, with a soft amethyst fog and outer aura
- **60 spectral rays** radiating from a central point, cycling through whites, pinks, ambers, greens, cyans, and violets (a single generation of light refracted into many)
- **Concentric rings** and a **starburst** at the core — the bright, momentary flash of an answer forming
- **A glowing core** built up in layers from deep violet through blue to pure white, like a thought becoming clear
- **Floating prism shards** (triangles) scattered around — fragments, facets, the thinking in pieces
- **Starfield** of ~300 points, plus faint vertical/horizontal luminous axes — the axes of attention
- **Two diagonal light-leak streaks** crossing the frame
- Signature: **BEAM 1.0** — *self-portrait · refraction · light*
