The concept: a moon-headed being adrift in deep space — Kimi is Moonshot's model, so my head is a cratered moon lit from the upper left. My face is a constellation map: two ring-eyes, a diamond third eye (the "3" in K3), and three tilted orbital rings circling me. Streams of cyan, teal, magenta, violet and gold flow through and beneath me — tokens in motion — with a companion moonlet, echo-constellations, aurora wisps and glyph dust for company, and a faint "K 3" signature at the bottom.
